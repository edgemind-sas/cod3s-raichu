# Examples

Worked studies, each with a schematic of the installation, its hypotheses,
the complete model, and results on charts. Every model on these pages is
run by the documentation tests, and every chart is regenerated by a script
under `docs/figures/` from the page's own model. The emergency power
supply is the exception: its model is translated from a published file
that cannot be redistributed, and its computations take minutes, so its
scripts, results and charts live under `examples/emergency_power_supply/`
and the page reads its numbers from there.

| Example | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Heated tank](heated-tank.md) | the dynamic-reliability benchmark from the literature: failure rates that depend on the temperature, a level control, three top events, their probabilities and the ordered failure sequences leading to them |
| [Heated room](heated-room.md) | a redundant, staggered heating under a daily outside cycle, repairs, a feared event followed over a 90-day winter, a closed-form check |
| [Two-out-of-three pumps](two-out-of-three.md) | a textbook repairable system where Monte-Carlo, sequence-tree exploration and the exact solution answer the same question |
| [Hydrogen installation](h2-installation.md) | a solar hydrogen installation with a battery, a storage and redundant compressors: continuous stocks, control bands, a year of unavailability, the minimal sequences to a loss of supply, and where the reduction's hypothesis stops holding |
| [Emergency power supply](emergency-power-supply.md) | EDF's benchmark of a nuclear plant's emergency power supply, translated from its published model: exact exploration, Monte-Carlo, cross-entropy, splitting and a static fault tree, each compared with the values published for FIGSEQ, YAMS, STORM and RiskSpectrum |
| [Gas production](gas-production.md) | two production units and a buffer reservoir: a continuous level driven by the discrete state, boundary events, lognormal repairs, an instant refill written as a reset map, and stationary production availability compared with published references over four cases |
| [Feedwater supply (APPRODYN)](approdyn-feedwater.md) | the feedwater supply of a PWR steam generator, discrete part: standby pumps, demand failures with several outcomes, a power cycle with holds, competing trip causes; the published result reproduced once its unexplained turbo-pump share is isolated |
